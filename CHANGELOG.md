# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-23

First release.

- Phone layout for the OJS 3.5 back end: slide-in ☰ menu, collapsible workflow stage bar, pages and dialogs that fit the screen.
- Tables, submission lists, plugin lists and Users & Roles shown as labelled cards, with the user name in bold on top and each start date next to its role.
- Tabs (journal, website, workflow and distribution settings, profile, site administration): one row that scrolls sideways and stays below the header, with the active tab joined to its panel. A sideways swipe, also slightly slanted and also on the grey page below a short panel, opens the next or previous tab, inner tabs first. The coloured line stays only on the open tab, and the focus dot appears only with the keyboard.
- Fewer boxes inside boxes: Tasks panel, Counter R5 Reports, Announcements, Institutions, statistics and lists inside settings tabs fill their space, without repeated titles; the Export button sits on the title line.
- Editorial Activity: a ring sized to the screen, with the stages listed beside it; Trends rows show the name on top and the figures side by side.
- Statistics charts: fewer dates under the chart, without the year when they share it, so the labels do not overlap.
- Side panels with a form, new or older (Hosted Journals > Edit, Metadata, Filters, Create Section, Navigation > Edit…): labels and fields keep a margin from the edge of the screen. Fields that took part of the width (Profile and other older forms) use the whole width, one per line.
- Grids inside side tabs (Workflow Settings > Components and the like): buttons stay inside the header and rows use the full width.
- Navigation menu editor: Assigned and Unassigned one below the other, each opening and closing with a tap on its title.
- DOIs: filters above the list, no title repeating the tab name. Invite to a role: the step bar fits the screen.
- No changes above 900 px; site-wide plugin with no settings.
- English and Italian localizations.
